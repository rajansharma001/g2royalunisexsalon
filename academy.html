02 Learning Path

How You Learn at G2 Royal

A step-by-step practical progression designed to build confidence on real clients.

01

Tools & Theory

Understanding scissor blades, clipper guards, hair anatomy, and strict tool sanitation.

02

Mannequin Practice

Practicing sectioning, holding angles, clean parting, and basic cut patterns repeatedly on dummy heads.

03

Supervised Models

Cutting live volunteers with an instructor standing right by your side to check every section.

04

Working Salon Floor

Handling everyday clients, managing salon timing, and developing real-world confidence.
