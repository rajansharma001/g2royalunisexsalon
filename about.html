01 About Us

About G2 Royal
Salon & Academy

G2 Royal Unisex Salon & Training Academy is a beauty salon and skills-training business located near Chakhu Mandir, Bafal Marg in Kathmandu. We combine everyday salon services with practical beauty and grooming education for both personal grooming clients and aspiring beauty professionals.

02 Our Setup

Everyday Salon Services & Practical Training

G2 Royal Unisex Salon & Training Academy operates in the Bafal area of Kathmandu, along Bafal Marg near Chakhu Mandir. We offer everyday hair, beard, and skin care services to the neighborhood, while simultaneously teaching students who want to build careers in the beauty and barbering industry.

Our approach is grounded in practicality. Students train inside a working salon rather than sitting only in a classroom. They watch experienced staff handle real customers, learn tool care and hygiene, and practice techniques under active supervision.

03 What We Offer

Salon Services & Training Courses

Clear services for clients and comprehensive training tracks for students.

Men's Grooming

Classic haircuts, scissor work, fade haircuts, beard shaping, warm towel shaves, and everyday styling.

Women's Hair & Color

Haircuts, split-end trims, blowdry styling, root touch-up, full coloring, highlights, and keratin smoothening.

Facials & Head Massage

Cleansing facials, scrubs, steam, blackhead removal, face packs, and relaxing head and shoulder massages.

Practical Skills Training

Short and multi-month courses in barbering, hairstyling, and basic beauty care taught inside our working salon.
